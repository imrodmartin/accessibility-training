Exercise 7.1 — Reviewing a real post

7 problems · find them first

How this works

Below is a short announcement from Riverside Public Works, exactly as it was posted. It has several problems. Find them before you open the answers.

  1. Read it once as a resident who wants to know when their leaves get picked up.
  2. Read it again as a screen reader user: headings only, then the links list, then the images.
  3. Stuck? Choose Show how this page is built inside the post. Hover or Tab to any piece to see its real tag, its alt text, and what a screen reader announces.
  4. List what is wrong, most serious first, then open the answers and compare your order.

Riverside Public Works News and notices

Leaf Collection

Important Notice

The leaf collection program starts soon. Zone assignments have changed. Click here for more info. For the map, click here. Questions? Click here.

Status: Zone 1 Zone 2 Zone 3 Zone 4

Your review

What’s wrong, in order of severity

Open when you have your list
  1. Text in an image. All four schedules exist only inside flyer.jpg. This is the most serious failure on the page, because the actual information is unavailable to a screen reader user, a translation tool, and anyone zooming on a phone. Nothing about the rest matters if the reader can’t get the dates. WCAG 1.1.1 Non-text Content (A) · 1.4.5 Images of Text (AA)
  2. Three links reading “click here”, each going to a different destination. The links list reads “click here, click here, click here.” WCAG 2.4.4 Link Purpose in Context (A)
  3. “Important Notice” is a fake heading, bolded rather than marked as a heading, so the page’s outline is one heading deep. WCAG 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (A)
  4. Color-only status. Green and red dots with no text label. WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color (A)
  5. “Starts soon” instead of a date. The one fact a reader needs is missing. Plain language · Section 508 program requirement
  6. ALL CAPS heading. Minor, but it slows everyone down and some screen readers spell out short capitalized strings. Readability; supports WCAG 3.1.5 Reading Level (AAA)
  7. No alt text on the flyer image, though as noted, alt text isn’t the fix here. WCAG 1.1.1 Non-text Content (A)

Seven problems in five sentences. Every one of them was a choice made in the editor, and every one has a fix that takes less time than typing the post did.
