⚠ 3 issues
Error. Please check your entries.

⚠ The error tells no one anything

Training annotation — not part of the form under review.

  1. No field is named. "Please check your entries" doesn't say which entry. A sighted user scans for red boxes; a screen reader user has to tab the whole form again to guess. Fails WCAG 3.3.1 Error Identification (Level A)
  2. Nothing is announced. The banner is injected with no role="alert" or live region, and focus stays on the button, so assistive tech says nothing at all — the form just appears not to submit. Fails WCAG 4.1.3 Status Messages (Level AA)
  3. No correction is offered. A rejected date or phone format gets the same eight words as a blank field. Fails WCAG 3.3.3 Error Suggestion (Level AA)
⚠ 2 issues

Fields in red are required.

⚠ "In red" is not information

Training annotation — not part of the form under review.

  1. Color is the only signal. Required status is carried purely by hue. Anyone with a color vision deficiency, a monochrome display, or a screen reader gets no indication at all. Fails WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color (Level A)
  2. Nothing is required in the markup. No required or aria-required="true" on any input, so assistive tech can't relay what the sentence claims. Fails WCAG 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions (Level A)
⚠ 4 issues

⚠ These boxes have no labels

Training annotation — not part of the form under review.

  1. Placeholder is not a label. There is no <label> anywhere in this form. Some screen readers read a placeholder, some don't, and none treat it as a name — so a field may be announced only as "edit text, blank". Fails WCAG 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (Level A) and 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions (Level A)
  2. The label vanishes on first keystroke. Once you type, the field's name is gone. Reviewing your answers, getting interrupted, or coming back to fix an error means re-deriving what each box was for. Fails WCAG 3.3.2; a documented barrier for users with cognitive and memory disabilities
  3. The gray fails contrast. Placeholder gray #a0a0a0 on white measures 2.61:1 — the minimum for body text is 4.5:1. Fails WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA)
  4. No autocomplete. Name, email, phone, and address fields carry no autocomplete attribute, so browsers can't fill them and users who rely on that must type every character. Fails WCAG 1.3.5 Identify Input Purpose (Level AA)
