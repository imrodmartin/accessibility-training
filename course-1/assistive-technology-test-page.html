Riverside Aquatics Center

An eight-lane outdoor lap pool and a zero-depth leisure pool at Heron Point Park, open year round.

Notice: the facility is closed Thursday, November 27 for Thanksgiving. Regular hours resume Friday at 6:00 a.m.

Daily admission is $4 for residents and $6 for non-residents. Children under 3 swim free.

Swim lessons

Six-week sessions begin September 14 and October 26. Choose an age group.

Ages 3 to 5. A caregiver is in the water for the first level. Saturdays at 9:00 a.m., $55 for six weeks.

Ages 6 to 12, levels 1 through 6. Mondays and Wednesdays at 4:30 p.m., $70 for six weeks.

Beginners and returning swimmers. Tuesdays at 7:00 p.m., $85 for six weeks.

Lap swim

Eight lanes are open during lap pool hours. Members can hold a lane for 60 minutes, up to seven days ahead.

    Accessibility at the pool

    Every pool has a zero-depth entry or a lift. An accessible family changing room has a roll-in shower and an adult-size changing table. Aquatic wheelchairs are free at the front desk.
