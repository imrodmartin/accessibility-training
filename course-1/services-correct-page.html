Our Services

The Department of Parks & Recreation maintains public green space, runs year-round programming, and supports community events across Riverside.

Park Maintenance

Mowing, tree care, playground inspection, and trail upkeep across 41 city parks and more than 300 acres of public land.

More about park maintenance

Youth Programs

After-school clubs, summer day camps, and school-break activities for children ages 5 through 17, with sliding-scale fees.

More about youth programs

Facility Rentals

Shelters, ball fields, meeting rooms, and the Willow Bend Amphitheater are available for private and community events.

More about facility rentals

Senior Recreation

Fitness classes, day trips, and social programming for residents 55 and older at the Riverbank Community Center.

More about senior recreation

Aquatics

Two outdoor pools and one indoor lap pool, with swim lessons, lifeguard certification, and open-swim hours.

More about aquatics

Volunteer Opportunities

Trail crews, park cleanups, coaching, and event staffing. Individual and group placements are available year-round.

More about volunteer opportunities

What changed from the inaccessible version
  1. The menus open on click, Enter or Space. Each top-level item that has a submenu is now a <button> with aria-expanded and aria-controls. The submenu is hidden until the button opens it, then every link in it is in the tab order. Tab through this page and count the links you can reach: all of them. Satisfies 2.1.1 Keyboard (A)
  2. Nothing appears on hover alone, so nothing vanishes on hover. An open menu stays open until you choose a link, press Escape, move focus out of it, or click elsewhere. Escape also returns focus to the button that opened it. Satisfies 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus (AA)
  3. Every link says where it goes. "Read more" six times became "More about park maintenance", "More about youth programs", and so on. A screen reader's list of links now reads as a table of contents. Satisfies 2.4.4 Link Purpose (In Context) (A) and 2.4.9 Link Purpose (Link Only) (AAA)
  4. Contrast was measured, not guessed. Body text is now #23303a on white, 13.5:1. Links are #145e78, 7.2:1, and underlined so color is not the only cue. Satisfies 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (AA) and 1.4.1 Use of Color (A)
  5. Focus is visible everywhere. The *:focus { outline: none; } rule is gone. A 3px ring with a 2px gap shows on every stop: navy on the white page and yellow on the navy and teal bars, where it keeps 3:1 against the background. Satisfies 2.4.7 Focus Visible (AA) and 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA)
  6. A skip link and landmarks were added. The header, navigation, main content and footer are real landmarks, and the first Tab stop lets a keyboard user bypass the menu bar. Satisfies 2.4.1 Bypass Blocks (A)
