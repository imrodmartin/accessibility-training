Riverside Parks and Recreation
Fall 2026 Program Guide
September 8 through December 19, 2026. Registration opens Monday, August 17.

Welcome to the Season

Fall is our busiest season on the river. This guide covers everything the department runs between Labor Day and the winter break, from toddler tumbling to the Turkey Trot. If you can't find what you're looking for, call the Recreation Office and we'll help you sort it out. Financial assistance is available for every program listed here.

Registration Information

How to Register

  • Online at riversideparks.gov/register, open 24 hours a day
  • By phone at (555) 402-1188 during office hours
  • In person at the Recreation Office, 410 Mill Street

Fees and Refunds

  • Residents pay the listed fee. Non-residents add $15 per program.
  • Full refunds are issued up to seven days before the first class.
  • Scholarships cover up to 75 percent of any program fee.

Youth Programs

Little Sprouts Play Group, Ages 2 to 4

Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9:30 to 11:00 a.m., Mill Street Community Room. $60 for the season. Caregivers stay on site. Snack provided.

Fall Youth Soccer, Grades K through 6

Saturday mornings at Cottonwood Fields beginning September 12. $85 including jersey. Volunteer coaches are needed for every age bracket.

After School Adventure Club, Grades 3 through 8

Monday through Friday, 3:15 to 6:00 p.m., Riverside Rec Center. $220 per month. Homework hour, gym time, and a weekly outdoor trip.

Adult and Family Programs

Coed Volleyball League

Wednesday evenings, 6:30 to 9:30 p.m., Rec Center gym. $250 per team, eight-week season. Team registration closes September 2.

Trailside Yoga

Saturdays at 8:00 a.m. through October 25, Heron Point Pavilion. $10 drop-in. Bring your own mat. Class moves indoors when it rains.

Family Campfire Nights

Second Friday of each month, 7:00 p.m., Riverside Campground. Free. Firewood, cocoa, and a naturalist talk are included.

Aquatics Center

Swim Lessons

Six-week sessions begin September 14 and October 26. Levels 1 through 6. $70 per session, small class sizes.

Open and Lap Swim

Lap swim runs weekdays 5:30 to 8:00 a.m. Open swim runs weekends 1:00 to 5:00 p.m. $4 daily or $65 for a season pass.

Active Adults, 55 and Better

Chair fitness meets Monday and Wednesday at 10:00 a.m. Cards and coffee run every Tuesday at 1:00 p.m. The Thursday day-trip bus visits a different county park each week, with lunch included for $18. All Active Adults programs are free to Riverside residents unless noted.

Shelter and Facility Rentals

Picnic shelters rent for $45 per half day and $75 per full day. The Rec Center gym and the Mill Street Community Room are available evenings and weekends starting at $60 per hour. Reservations open twelve months in advance and require a signed use agreement.
