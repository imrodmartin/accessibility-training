Our Services

The Department of Parks & Recreation maintains public green space, runs year-round programming, and supports community events across Riverside.

Park Maintenance

Mowing, tree care, playground inspection, and trail upkeep across 41 city parks and more than 300 acres of public land.

Read more

Youth Programs

After-school clubs, summer day camps, and school-break activities for children ages 5 through 17, with sliding-scale fees.

Read more

Facility Rentals

Shelters, ball fields, meeting rooms, and the Willow Bend Amphitheater are available for private and community events.

Read more

Senior Recreation

Fitness classes, day trips, and social programming for residents 55 and older at the Riverbank Community Center.

Read more

Aquatics

Two outdoor pools and one indoor lap pool, with swim lessons, lifeguard certification, and open-swim hours.

Read more

Volunteer Opportunities

Trail crews, park cleanups, coaching, and event staffing. Individual and group placements are available year-round.

⚠ Training notes — five failures on this page
  1. The menu opens on hover only. Submenus are display: none until li:hover fires. That also removes their links from the tab order entirely, so a keyboard user never reaches "Find a Park" or "Swim Lessons" — the items are not just hard to get to, they are unreachable. Touch users are stranded the same way. Tab through this page and count how many links you can get to. Fails WCAG 2.1.1 Keyboard (Level A)
  2. The menu can't be dismissed or hovered into safely. Content that appears on hover must be dismissible, must stay open while the pointer moves onto it, and must persist until dismissed. This one vanishes the moment the pointer leaves the parent. Fails WCAG 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus (Level AA)
  3. Six links that all say "Read more." Screen reader users routinely pull up a list of every link on a page to navigate. Here that list reads: Read more, Read more, Read more, Read more, Read more, Read more. The heading above each one is not programmatically part of the link's context, so the destination cannot be determined from the link or its context. Fails WCAG 2.4.4 Link Purpose (In Context) (Level A); also fails 2.4.9 Link Purpose (Link Only) (Level AAA)
  4. Light gray body text and even lighter links. Body copy #aaaaaa on white measures 2.32:1 and the "Read more" links #8fa8b5 measure 2.49:1. The minimum for body text is 4.5:1. Fails WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA)
  5. Focus outlines removed site-wide. A single *:focus { outline: none; } rule deletes the only cue a sighted keyboard user has for where they are on the page. Tab through this page and watch nothing happen. Fails WCAG 2.4.7 Focus Visible (Level AA)
