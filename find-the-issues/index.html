How this works

Below is a department email announcement. It looks completely normal. Nine accessibility issues are embedded in it. Find as many as you can before you open the answers.

  1. Hover over any part of the email — or press Tab to the numbered button beside it — and the panel on the right tells you how that piece is actually built: the tag, the font, the color, the alt text.
  2. Decide from that description alone whether the formatting is sound. No browser inspector, no scanner, no code view needed.
  3. Flag the ones you think are broken by clicking the numbered button. Thirteen items are marked. Nine are problems. Four are fine — flagging those costs you.
  4. When you are done, choose Check my answers.
