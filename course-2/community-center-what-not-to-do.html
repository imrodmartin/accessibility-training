Community Center Reopening

The Department of Parks and Recreation is pleased to announce that, following the completion of the capital improvement project authorized by the City Council in the 2025 budget cycle and undertaken in phases over the preceding fourteen months, during which time the facility remained closed to the public in the interest of safety, the Riverside Community Center, including the gymnasium, the pool, the fitness room and all meeting rooms, will resume regular operations and reopen its doors to residents and members of the public on Monday, October 6.

What’s new

  • A second gym court with a walking track above it
  • An accessible family changing room beside the pool
  • Automatic doors at every public entrance
  • A quiet room off the main lobby

Hours

Hours are subject to change on holidays.
