Fall Registration Opens September 15

Registration for fall programs opens Monday, September 15 at 8:00 a.m. Youth programs fill quickly, so have your account ready before then.

Register for a fall program. Fees are due at registration; read the fee and refund policy. Scholarships cover up to 75 percent of a program fee. Find out whether you qualify for a scholarship, then download the scholarship application (PDF, 240 KB).

Volunteer

We need youth soccer coaches for the fall season. No experience required; we train you, and your own child plays free. Volunteer as a youth soccer coach.

Aquatics Center rules

Lap swim and Learn to Swim take place at the Riverside Aquatics Center. Swim caps are required in the lap lanes and children under 8 must be accompanied in the water. Read the Aquatics Center rules.

Last year

In 2025 we ran 48 programs, served 2,140 residents, and 31 percent of participants enrolled on a scholarship.

All youth programs meet the state’s licensing standards. Read Indiana’s youth program licensing standards.
