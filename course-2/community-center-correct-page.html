The Community Center reopens Monday, October 6

After fourteen months of renovation, the gym, pool, fitness room and all meeting rooms open again on Monday, October 6.

What’s new

  • A second gym court with a walking track above it
  • An accessible family changing room beside the pool
  • Automatic doors at every public entrance
  • A quiet room off the main lobby
