Leaf and Yard Waste Collection

Leaf collection runs November 2 through December 31, 2026. We collect every two weeks by zone. Zone assignments changed this year, so check your zone before your first collection day.

Zone assignments changed this year

Westfield joined the City on July 1. Westfield addresses are now in Zone 3 and are collected on Wednesdays. All other zone boundaries are unchanged.

How to bag and set out your leaves

  1. Use paper yard-waste bags only. Plastic bags are not collected.
  2. Get free paper bags at the Public Works yard, 1200 Industrial Drive, or buy them at most hardware stores.
  3. Cut brush to four feet or shorter and tie it in bundles.
  4. Put bags and bundles at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your collection day. Keep them out of the street and off the sidewalk.

Crews make one pass through each zone. They do not come back for material set out after the truck has gone by. If you miss your day, keep your bags until your zone's next date. Do not rake loose leaves into the street.

What we accept

Accepted

  • Leaves
  • Grass clippings
  • Garden trimmings
  • Brush up to four feet long, bundled

Not accepted

  • Plastic bags
  • Soil, sod, and rocks
  • Lumber and construction debris
  • Household trash
  • Branches over two inches thick

Questions?

Call Public Works at (555) 013-2200, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., or email publicworks@riverside.example.gov.

Bags left in the street are an obstruction under Ordinance 2024-17 and may be cited. The fine for a first violation is $50; repeat violations in the same season carry higher fines. Read the ordinance at the City Clerk's office or on the City website.
