The situation

This is the real test. A colleague has drafted the Community Center reopening announcement and asked you to look before it goes live. Nine things are wrong. Two more are wrong that we’re not counting; finding those is extra credit. Four of the marked items are fine, and flagging those costs you.

  1. Run the pre-publish checklist cold: headings, plain language, links, images, tables, color.
  2. Flag anything you would send back by clicking its numbered button. Stuck on one? Choose Show how this page is built inside the draft and hover or Tab to it.
  3. When you are satisfied, choose Publish. Whatever you missed goes live, and Monday’s inbox tells you how that went. Every message comes with the fix.
  4. Then choose Run the automated checker and count how many of the nine it caught. That gap is the reason this course exists.

Community Center Reopening

The Department of Parks and Recreation is pleased to announce that, following the completion of the capital improvement project authorized by the City Council in the 2025 budget cycle and undertaken in phases over the preceding fourteen months, during which time the facility remained closed to the public in the interest of safety, the Riverside Community Center, including the gymnasium, the pool, the fitness room and all meeting rooms, will resume regular operations and reopen its doors to residents and members of the public on Monday, October 6.
