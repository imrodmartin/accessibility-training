    What changed from the inaccessible version
    • Every field has a real <label> tied by for and id. Labels stay on screen while you type, so nothing disappears on the first keystroke. Satisfies 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (A) and 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions (A)
    • Required fields say "(required)" in text and carry required plus aria-required="true". Color is no longer carrying any meaning on its own. Satisfies 1.4.1 Use of Color (A)
    • Errors name the field and appear beside it, with a summary at the top that links to each one. The summary is a role="alert" region and takes focus on submit, so screen reader users hear the problem immediately. Satisfies 3.3.1 Error Identification (A) and 4.1.3 Status Messages (AA)
    • Error text says how to fix it — "Enter an email address in the format name@example.com", not "check your entries". Satisfies 3.3.3 Error Suggestion (AA)
    • Name, email, phone and address fields carry autocomplete, so the browser can fill them. Satisfies 1.3.5 Identify Input Purpose (AA)
    • Contrast was measured, not guessed. Body text is 13.5:1, hint text 7.1:1, input borders 4.4:1 against white. Satisfies 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (AA) and 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA)
    • These notes are a disclosure, not a hover tooltip. The earlier version's bubble vanished on pointer-out and could not be dismissed. Satisfies 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus (AA)
    • A skip link, landmarks and one h1 let keyboard and screen reader users bypass the header on every visit. Satisfies 2.4.1 Bypass Blocks (A)
