How the training runs

We run the sessions against your own pages wherever we can. An audit tells you what is broken; this training hands your team the checks that keep it fixed.

Sessions cap at 20 participants so everyone gets time in the exercise pages. Recordings, exercise files and the remediation checklist stay with your team afterward.
